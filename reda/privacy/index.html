Reda Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Reda iOS app (bundle ID com.reda.sdeevc, "Reda", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Reda helps you plan themed city strolls, capture street frames and short clips against a task checklist, log your walking time and rests, and turn a finished stroll into a vertical walk reel and a stroll card.

If you have questions, contact us at RedaSession@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Account information

Reda uses Quick Login. When you tap the start button on the welcome page, the app generates a random username (for example flaneur_ab12cd) and a random password for your Reda account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any social login. The generated credentials are kept by the app so you can return to the same account after logging out.

1.2 Content you create

When you use Reda, the app stores the content you create for your account:

  • An optional profile photo you pick from your photo library in Settings, shown as your account photo in Reda.
  • Stroll plans: theme titles, street notes, timeframes, district names, task prompts, and custom prompts you type.
  • Walk log records: start, pause, resume, and finish times; rest entries and their names; typed or dictated street notes.
  • Photos and short video clips you capture with the camera or import from your photo library, plus their thumbnails and the task they are attached to.
  • Audio memos and voiceovers you record.
  • Reel studio settings (template, title, audio options), free draft previews, exported reels, cover images, and stroll cards.

Your strolls, media, and reels are not published to other users. They are shown inside your own account (for example in your Journal, Missions, Studio, and Archive) until you choose to save or share an exported file using the iOS share sheet or the Photos app.

1.3 Credits and purchases

Reda sells consumable Walk Reel Credits through Apple In-App Purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your card number or Apple ID password. For each purchase the app records the credit lot, number of credits, Apple's transaction identifier, and the time, so that credits are added exactly once and your balance and credit history are accurate. When you export a reel, the app records the credits used for that export.

1.4 Information we do not collect

Reda does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, health data, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints. Reda contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the features you ask for:

  • Run your account (Quick Login, Log Out, Delete Account).
  • Plan, log, and review strolls; attach media to tasks; play back audio memos.
  • Render draft previews and exported reels, and create stroll cards.
  • Add purchased credits and deduct credits when an export finishes.
  • Respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Device permissions

Reda asks for each permission only when you tap the feature that needs it. You can change any permission later in the iOS Settings app.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Reda does not use Face ID, the TrueDepth camera, face detection, face recognition, or face landmarks, and it does not create, store, or share any faceprint, face geometry, or other biometric identifier. Photos and video clips that you capture or import for your stroll tasks, and the profile photo you pick, are ordinary images and may show people's faces. Reda does not analyze those images for faces.

4.2 How we use it

Photos and clips (including any faces that appear in them) are used only to show them in your Missions checklist and Media Vault, to set a cover frame, to compose your draft previews and exported walk reels, and to draw your stroll card. Your profile photo is used only to show your account photo at the top of Reda's screens, in Settings, and on your own Journal reel cards. None of these images are used for identification, profiling, advertising, or training any model.

4.3 Sharing and where it is stored

Photos and clips you add, and your profile photo, are stored inside the Reda app's storage on your iPhone for your account. They are not uploaded to us and are not shared with any third party. They leave the app only when you choose to save a reel or stroll card to Photos or share an exported file through the iOS share sheet.

4.4 Retention and deletion

Photos and clips are kept until you delete them. Your profile photo is kept until you pick another one, choose Use Default Photo in Settings, or delete your account. You can delete a single item from the Media Vault or a task attachment viewer, delete a whole stroll from the Archive, use Clear All Stroll Data in the Archive, or use Settings → Delete Account, which removes all of your photos, clips, previews, reels, and your profile photo from Reda. Copies you saved to the Photos app or shared elsewhere are managed outside Reda.

5. Third-party services

Reels, previews, and stroll cards are rendered by the app on your iPhone using Apple frameworks. The services Reda relies on are provided by Apple: App Store and In-App Purchase, the Speech framework for dictation (see Section 3), the Photos framework, and the notification service.

6. Contacting us

If you tap Settings → Contact Support, the app opens your mail app with a pre-filled message to RedaSession@gmail.com that includes your Reda username. We receive that message, together with your email address, only if you send it. We use support emails only to answer you, and we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Log Out ends your session. Your strolls, reels, and credits remain in your account so you can continue after tapping the button on the welcome page again.
  • Delete a stroll (Archive → more actions → Delete Stroll) removes that stroll and its snaps, clips, voice memos, previews, and exported reels.
  • Clear All Stroll Data (Archive) removes every stroll and its files and your credit ledger, including any unused credits. Your account remains signed in.
  • Delete Account (Settings → Delete Account) permanently removes your generated credentials, session, strolls, media, audio, previews, reels, and credit ledger and unused credits. Reda then returns to its first-launch state. Built-in sample content that ships with the app remains available.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Purchases remain in Apple's records of your App Store transactions.

8. Children

Reda is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used Reda, contact us and we will help remove the data.

9. Security

We use the protections provided by iOS, including app isolation and Data Protection encryption, to keep your Reda data separate from other apps. No method of storage is completely secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

10. Your choices and rights

You can review, edit, and delete your content at any time inside the app, change permissions in the iOS Settings app, and delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. To exercise them, email RedaSession@gmail.com.
